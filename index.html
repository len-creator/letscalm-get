Install on Android

Google Play requires two quick steps for beta apps. Use the same Google account that is signed in on your phone for both.

  1. Join the LetsCalm testers group

    Tap Join group on the page that opens. This is what tells Google Play you are a tester.

    Join the testers group
  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Give it a minute after joining, then tap Become a tester. The page will then show an install link.

    Opt in on Google Play
  3. Install LetsCalm from Google Play

    Please keep it installed while you're testing so the beta can proceed.
