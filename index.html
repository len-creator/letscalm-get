Know when your pet is stressed, and help them settle.

LetsCalm runs on your phone and listens for the sounds that mean your cat or dog is not okay. When it hears them, it plays calming audio and can let you know by email. Every sound is analyzed on the device itself; nothing is recorded or uploaded. PRIVATE BETA

How it works

1

It listens

A tiny, low-power model checks the microphone a few times a second for pet vocalizations. Everything else, from your conversations to the TV, is ignored and discarded immediately.

2

It understands

When a pet is heard, a larger on-device model reads the mood of a cat's vocalization, and measures how persistent a dog's barking is. The app shows a live attention level so you can see how your pet is doing.

3

It responds

Sustained distress triggers a gentle pattern interrupt: a calming track, a purr, or your own recorded voice. Optionally, it emails a verified address so you know while you're out.

Privacy by design

  • Audio never leaves your phone. Sound is turned into numbers in memory, analyzed, and thrown away. There is no recording and no upload.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking in the app. The app works without signing up and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. This website uses Google Analytics for visit counts only.
  • Alerts are opt in. Email alerts only exist if you enter an address and confirm it with a code, and you can switch them off at any time.
  • Crash reports are technical only. If the app fails, it sends a stack trace and device details so we can fix it, never audio or personal details.

The full detail is in our privacy policy.
